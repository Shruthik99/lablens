# Ambiguous drug names: RxNorm terms that are also ordinary words in discharge documents.
#
# These are NOT deleted from the lexicon. Many are real medications (iron, oxygen,
# potassium). They are flagged so the extractor only emits them when the surrounding
# text shows clinical context: a dose, a route, or a medication verb nearby.
# See docs/decisions/0001-rxnorm-lexicon-ambiguity.md.
#
# Inclusion rule: the term commonly appears in a discharge document in a NON-medication
# sense (lab panel, vital sign, diet advice, material, device). Terms that are only ever
# drugs (niacin, biotin, tylenol) are deliberately absent: flagging them would cost recall
# for no precision gain.
#
# Every entry below was verified to exist in RxNorm IN/PIN/BN (version 02-Nov-2026).
# One name per line, lowercase. Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
